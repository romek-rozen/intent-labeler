## Use
- Category winner table (daily trainer, beginner, max cushion, stability, speed) with prices: Lets searchers jump to the sub-need that matches their use case
- Per-shoe pros/cons list: Enables quick trade-off comparison between models
- At-a-glance model comparison with runner-type fit notes: Helps match a model to the runner's level, distance, and support needs
- Price ranges per model: Answers budget filtering directly

## Avoid
- Brand-only marketing page presented as a neutral roundup: Searchers want cross-brand comparison, not a single-brand pitch
- Purely anecdotal peer replies without specs or prices: The AI Overview and editorial results set an expectation of concrete model names, categories, and prices
