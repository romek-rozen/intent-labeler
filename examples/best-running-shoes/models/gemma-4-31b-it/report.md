- **Find best overall running shoes** - The searcher wants a curated list of the highest-rated running shoes across various categories to make a purchase decision.

- **Evaluate specific shoe brands** - The searcher wants to know which brands are reliable or how a specific brand's lineup performs.
- **Learn how to choose running shoes** - The searcher is looking for the criteria and logic used to select the right shoe for their specific foot and running style.
