## Use
- Categorized Product List: Allows users to self-select based on their primary need (e.g., 'I'm a beginner' or 'I need max cushion').

## Avoid
- Generic 'Top 10' without categorization: Searchers have diverse needs (trail, speed, stability); a flat list forces them to read everything to find relevance.
- Outdated models: Running shoe tech changes rapidly; results show 2026 titles and v15/v9 versions, implying high sensitivity to recency.
