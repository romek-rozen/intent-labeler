## Use
- Agency comparison table: Let searchers compare service focus, London relevance, specialties, and contact or audit options.
- Selection checklist: Help prospective clients assess evidence, reporting, technical capability, local experience, and communication.
- Pricing and budget context: Address affordability and the prominent questions about London SEO agency costs.
- Service and process summary: Clarify what an agency does, from audit and technical fixes through content and authority building.

## Avoid
- Generic SEO explainer with no provider-selection help: The results mostly serve people looking for London agencies or ways to assess and contact them.
- Unsupported rankings or guaranteed-result claims: Searchers need credible ways to judge providers, and the selection guide emphasizes evidence and transparent reporting.
- An AI-search-only treatment: AI SEO is represented by one specialist result, while the broader result set centers on conventional agency services and hiring.
