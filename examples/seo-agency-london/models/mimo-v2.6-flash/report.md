## Use
- Free SEO audit / free quote form: Lets searchers start a conversation with an agency at low commitment
- Selection criteria checklist (7 things to check): Helps searchers vet agencies before signing a contract
- Numbered best-of list with specs per agency: Enables quick comparison across multiple agencies
- Process step list (audit → fix → content): Shows how the agency actually works and what deliverables to expect
- PAA pricing questions: Answers budget concerns directly on the page

## Avoid
- Generic marketing prose with no service detail or pricing context: Searchers here are evaluating agencies to hire; vague content loses to concrete services, process, and pricing signals
- Guaranteed rankings claims: The buyer-guide result explicitly flags 'guaranteed page one' promises as a red flag searchers should check for
