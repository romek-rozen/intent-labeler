- **Replacement and retrofitting costs** - Understand the specific costs associated with replacing an old heating system with a heat pump.
- **General technology introduction** - Get a basic understanding of what a heat pump is and its general benefits/costs.

## Use
- Comparison Table for Heat Pump Types: Allows users to quickly see price differences between Air-Water, Ground-Source, and Water-Water systems.
- Subsidy Calculation Example: Shows the difference between gross cost and net cost after government funding (BEG), which is a major decision factor.
- Running Cost Calculator/Formula: Helps users estimate annual electricity bills based on household size and current energy tariffs.
- FAQ Section: Addresses specific questions from People Also Ask like 'costs for 120sqm house' or 'who should not buy one'.

## Avoid
- Vague marketing promises without numbers: Users searching for 'Kosten' want concrete figures (€ amounts), not just qualitative claims about efficiency.
- Ignoring subsidy impact: In Germany, the net cost is significantly lower than the gross cost due to state subsidies; omitting this makes the data misleading.
