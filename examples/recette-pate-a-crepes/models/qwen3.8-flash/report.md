## Use
- Ingredients table: Allows quick scanning of precise ratios (flour/milk/eggs).
- Numbered step list: Provides clear sequential actions for mixing and cooking.

- Resting time note: Clarifies if immediate cooking is possible or if waiting is required.

## Avoid
- Long personal stories: Searchers want the formula immediately; context should be minimal.
- Vague measurements: Crepe batter relies on liquid-to-flour ratios; 'a handful' causes failure.
