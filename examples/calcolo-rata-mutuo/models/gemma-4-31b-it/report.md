## Searcher goals
- **calculate monthly mortgage payment** - The user wants an interactive tool to quickly determine their monthly mortgage cost based on loan amount, duration, and interest rate.
- **understand mortgage calculation logic** - The user wants to know the formulas, rules, and factors (like French amortization or TAN/TAEG) used to calculate a mortgage payment.
- **find mortgage calculator apps** - The user is looking for software applications to install on a mobile device for calculating mortgages.
- **calculate mortgage expiration date** - The user wants to determine when their mortgage will end based on remaining debt and payment amount.
