- **calculate generic loan installments** - The user wants a tool to estimate the monthly cost of a loan based on amount, interest, and term.
- **calculate mortgage installments and capacity** - The user specifically wants to estimate mortgage payments and check how much they can borrow (credit capacity).
- **calculate impact of interest rate changes** - The user already has a loan and wants to see how a change in interest rates will affect their current payment.
