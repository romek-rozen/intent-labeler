Recommended elements

Use
  • Day-by-day timeline - Shows the evolution of the starter over the first two weeks.
  • Ingredient ratio table - Provides clear proportions for flour and water during refreshments.
  • Troubleshooting checklist - Helps users identify if the starter is 'too strong' or inactive.
Avoid
  • Quick-fix summaries - Creating a natural starter is a slow process; oversimplifying it may lead to failed fermentation.
