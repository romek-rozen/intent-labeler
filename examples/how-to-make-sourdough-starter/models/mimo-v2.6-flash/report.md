## Use
- Day-by-day timeline table (Day 1 through Day 7+): Lets the reader follow the process on the day they are on and see what to expect next.
- Ingredient/feeding ratio callout (flour:water by weight or volume): Answers the most common point of confusion at a glance — how much of each to feed.
- Equipment checklist: Lets the searcher gather jar, scale, cloth, flour, and water before starting.
- Readiness signs list (double in 4-6 hours, smell, bubbles): Tells the reader when the starter is done and usable.
- Troubleshooting / common beginner mistakes section: Covers PAA questions about mistakes and slow growth.
- Short embedded video clip of the initial mix: Serves the visual learners who currently land on Shorts/TikTok results.

## Avoid
- Pure product or kit sales page as the main answer: Related searches show buying intent exists, but the primary query is 'how to make', and a shop page would not satisfy the step-by-step need.
- Over-long bread-baking treatise before the starter steps: Searchers want the starter started now; burying Day 1 instructions delays the core task the AI Overview already delivers upfront.
- Vague narrative prose without exact quantities and temperatures: Every winning result leads with concrete ratios and temperatures; vagueness would fail against both the AI Overview and video snippets.
