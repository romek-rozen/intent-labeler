- Interactive Daily Schedule Table: Allows users to check off each day's feeding and observe the progression from liquid batter to bubbly starter.

## Avoid
- Vague 'magic' tips without science: Searchers are increasingly educated; they want to understand why whole wheat flour works better initially than all-purpose.
- Assuming access to a scale: Many top-ranking social results use cups; ignoring volume-based instructions alienates a significant portion of the audience.
