Recommended elements

Use
  • Day-by-day schedule - Shows when to mix, discard, and feed the starter throughout its development.
  • Ingredient measurements and ratios - Lets beginners reproduce the mixture reliably using the stated flour and water amounts.
  • Readiness checklist - Helps readers decide when the starter is active enough to use.
  • Progress photos or short video - Shows how the mixture and its activity change between feedings.
Avoid
  • A general sourdough history article - The results serve a hands-on starter-making task, not background research.
  • A bread recipe without starter-making instructions - The searcher needs to create and maintain the starter before using it to bake.
