## Use
- Comparison table: Allows users to quickly scan specs like weight capacity, height range, and price across different models.
- Product cards with prices: Directly serves transactional intent by showing current costs and availability.
- Pros and cons list: Helps users evaluate trade-offs between stability, assembly difficulty, and cost.

## Avoid
- Purely theoretical health essays: Most searchers are in the consideration or purchase phase; they need actionable product info, not just general wellness advice.
- Unstructured text walls: Users comparing multiple products need scannable data points, not long paragraphs.
