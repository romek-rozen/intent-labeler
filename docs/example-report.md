# Intent report: standing desk

Mixed SERP: shoppers comparing models dominate, with health and setup questions as supporting intents.

- Dominant intent: **Buy a desk now** (40% of results)
- Form that answers it: shop category listing
- Genre the SERP expects: buying guide with model comparison
- Reference length: 1200 words / ~7200 chars (IQR 900-1500 words), basis: median_of_dominant_intent_pages, n=4

## Warnings
- **consider_separate_pages** - 3 intents hold at least 15% each. Consider separate pages instead of one.
- **traffic_disagrees** - By answers the dominant intent is "Buy a desk now", by traffic "Compare and choose a desk": many weak pages versus one strong page. Decide deliberately.

Traffic known for 8 of 10 results.

## Use
- comparison table of tested models: lets the reader shortlist by price, height range and motor
- height chart by user height: answers the setup question without leaving the page

## Avoid
- single-product sales page: the dominant searcher is still comparing

## Reader questions
- How high should a standing desk be?
- Are standing desks worth it?
